01

Real-time treasury dashboard

A finance team tracked its global cash position in a manual spreadsheet, rebuilt every morning from six disparate bank portals across four currencies.

The hard part
Synthesizing dense, multi-currency financial data into a single viewport from zero. We had to cure visual overload so a critical $2M floor breach wouldn't get buried under routine bank statements.
What we did
Engineered a comprehensive frontend portal. We anchored five macro-KPIs, built custom dynamic charts separating actual vs. projected liquidity, and implemented a severity-ranked alert system to instantly surface financial risks.
Outcome
Transformed treasury operations from reactive monthly audits to a real-time command center, enabling instant remediation of critical breaches while providing clear visibility into 30-day liquidity gaps.

02

Broker portal for SME lending

A lender took broker loan applications through email, spreadsheets and phone calls. Nobody could say where a deal sat, what it would price at, or why credit had pushed back — without asking a person.

The hard part
Two audiences, one screen. Brokers want a number they can quote today; credit wants the risk evidence behind it. Split them into separate tools and the deal stalls in the gap between them.
What we did
Designed and built it from zero: one application record carrying status, pricing and risk side by side. Company risk is stated as a plain verdict — high risk — with the factors that produced it, and every quote is shown as principal against interest over the full term, not a single monthly figure.
Outcome
Independently ranked by the Financial Times among the fastest-growing companies in Asia-Pacific two years running — 8th overall and 1st among fintechs in 2022, 9th overall and 2nd among fintechs in 2023.

Before you ask

Straight answers.

The eight questions we get on almost every first call.

What does it actually cost?

A Define sprint starts at A$18k fixed. A Build engagement typically lands between A$45k and A$120k depending on the slice. Evolve starts at A$14k per month. You get a written number before anything begins, and we would rather tell you on the first call that your budget does not fit than discover it in week three.

How fast can you start?

Because the team is small, we run a limited number of engagements at once. If we cannot start when you need us, we will say so immediately. A next Define slot opens in two weeks.

We already have designers and engineers. Why you?

Then you likely do not need capacity — you need a decision. We are usually brought in when a workflow has been redesigned twice and still is not right, or when nobody senior can hold the product and the technical constraints in one head at the same time. We work alongside in-house teams, and we hand over documented.

What happens if we stop after Define?

You keep everything: the maps, the prototype, the technical approach, the delivery plan and the risk list — in a form another team can pick up. Roughly the point of the sprint is to make that choice cheaply. No exit fee, no clawback.

Which stack do you work in?

Modern frontend (React and TypeScript) with full-stack delivery, and Figma for design. We are comfortable inside existing codebases, including older ones, and we will not propose a rewrite because it would be more pleasant for us.

Will you touch production data or PII?

Not by default. We work with synthetic or anonymised data, in your environment, under least-privilege access. Anything beyond that is agreed in writing, scoped and time-boxed. See Working with us for the full position.

Who owns the IP?

You do. Design files, source code and documentation are assigned to you on payment of each milestone, and they live in your repository and workspace from the first day rather than being handed over at the end.

Can we talk to a reference?

Yes, on request once we know your context, so we can connect you with someone who solved a comparable problem rather than whoever is easiest to reach.
